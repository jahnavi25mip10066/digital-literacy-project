social-media-checklist.md

Do's                                             	
1. Use privacy settings to control who sees your posts.
2. Think twice before posting anything permanent.	
3. Network with professionals in your field.	
4. Verify information before sharing news or links.	
5. Keep your academic and professional achievements updated.	

Don'ts
1. Don't post offensive or discriminatory content.
2. Don't share your location or private address publicly.
3. Don't engage in cyberbullying or online arguments.
4. Don't use unprofessional usernames or profile pics.
5. Don't tag people in posts without their permission.
